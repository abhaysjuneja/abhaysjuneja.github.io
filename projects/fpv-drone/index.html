FPV drone flown with mouse and keyboard

A quadcopter on Pixhawk 4 and ArduPilot whose attitude you control with a mouse and keyboard, like flying a plane in a video game, with a live camera feed.

When
2018
Where
North Carolina State University
Status
prototype

Problem

Drones are usually flown with a two-stick RC transmitter, which takes practice. Mouse-and-keyboard flight, familiar from video games, is a different way to give a pilot direct control of the aircraft’s attitude.

What I built

A first-person-view drone for the Introduction to Autonomous Systems class at NC State, flown from a computer with a mouse and keyboard.

How it works

  • Flight stack. A Pixhawk 4 flight controller running ArduPilot, with its full sensor suite, and a FlySky RC link.
  • Control. The mouse and keyboard set the drone’s attitude, similar to flying a plane in a video game.
  • Video. An onboard camera streams live video for first-person flight.
  • Ground station. Mission Planner as the ground control station.

Results

Built and flown for the Introduction to Autonomous Systems class at NC State in 2018.
