Perception for the Roxo delivery robot

Real-time detection, segmentation and multi-object tracking for FedEx's same-day sidewalk delivery robot, running on NVIDIA Jetson.

Problem

Roxo was FedEx’s same-day delivery robot, built to drive on sidewalks and cross streets on its own. That meant seeing traffic lights, people and obstacles reliably, in real time, on the compute that fits inside a small robot.

What I built

As part of DEKA’s Robot Perception/ML team:

  • Trained and deployed a multi-class RetinaNet object detector for traffic lights, running in real time on the NVIDIA Jetson platform with TensorRT.
  • Trained classification, segmentation and detection models on proprietary data and implemented the evaluation metrics used to judge them.
  • Evaluated deep learning approaches against the robot’s hardware limits before they went anywhere near the robot.
  • Built a distributed training pipeline with PyTorch and Determined AI.

How it works

Camera data feeds compact neural networks optimised with TensorRT for the Jetson. A semantic segmentation model was compressed up to 100x with little or no loss in accuracy, which is what made real-time inference possible.

On the multi-object tracking team, I implemented GNN, Hungarian and Joint Probabilistic data association, coupled with Kalman filters, to estimate the pose and velocity of objects around the robot.

Results

Real-time perception on embedded hardware, and a granted US patent: System and Method for Determining Object Characteristics in Real-time (US 12423987 B2), co-invented with Raajitha Gummadi.
