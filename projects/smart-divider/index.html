Smart dividers for road traffic congestion control

A road divider that shifts lanes toward the heavier traffic flow, driven by live traffic data in the cloud. Granted Indian patent 529030.

Where

Indian patent 529030, granted 2024

Problem

At peak hours, traffic on a road is often heavy in one direction and light in the other, yet the divider gives both directions the same number of lanes. Moving the divider to match the flow adds capacity where it is needed without widening the road.

What I built

A cloud-controlled divider prototype, together with Aditya Mukherjee, Daksh Gulati and Dr. L. Bhaskara Rao, the co-inventors on the patent.

  • A web app on GE Predix that pulls lane-level traffic events and sets the divider position.
  • Analysis of the traffic data in Python to separate heavy and light traffic.
  • Divider firmware on Arduino that reads the target position over Wi-Fi and moves the divider with stepper motors.

How it works

  • Traffic data. A Node.js app on Predix authenticates with UAA and reads traffic events from a Predix traffic API. Each event carries the lane, vehicle type, speed, direction and vehicle count.
  • Analysis. A Python script clusters the events by hour, speed and vehicle count with K-Means into two groups, heavy and light, and plots them as a bubble chart.
  • Control. The app exposes the divider state (left, center or right) at a /dividerStatus endpoint.
  • Divider. An Arduino with an ESP8266 Wi-Fi module polls that endpoint. When the state changes, two 4-phase stepper motors, front and back, move the divider one lane at a time.

Results

The invention, “Smart Dividers for Road Traffic Congestion Control for Smart Cities”, was filed in India in December 2016 and granted as Indian patent 529030 in March 2024.
