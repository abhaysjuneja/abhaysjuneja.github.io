Area-sweeping surveillance drone

A coverage algorithm that flies a DJI Mavic Pro over a field autonomously, with a cloud architecture on GE's Predix platform.

When
2017
Where
GE Digital
Role
IT Intern
Status
prototype
Ran on a DJI Mavic Pro

Problem

Surveying a field with a drone by hand is slow and leaves gaps. The goal was a drone that sweeps a given area on its own, with the data flowing into an industrial cloud platform.

What I built

  • An area-sweeping algorithm for an autonomous field surveillance drone.
  • An implementation on a DJI Mavic Pro using the DJI Mobile SDK for Android.
  • A cloud architecture for the complete system on Predix, GE’s industrial IoT platform.

How it works

The drone covers the area in back-and-forth passes, so every part of the field is seen. The Android app drives the flight through the DJI Mobile SDK, and the system design connects it to Predix in the cloud.

Results

A working sweep on a real DJI Mavic Pro, built during a six-month internship at GE Digital in Bengaluru.
