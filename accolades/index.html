Paper · IEEE CBS 2019, Munich

A Comparative Study of SLAM Algorithms for Indoor Navigation of Autonomous Wheelchairs

Compares five SLAM algorithms (Gmapping, Hector, RTAB-Map, VINS-Mono, RGBD-SLAM) on a wheelchair fitted with LiDAR, Kinect, IMU and wheel encoders. RTAB-Map proved the most scalable across sensor setups and speeds. With L. Bhandari, H. Mohammadbagherpoor, A. Singh and E. Grant. DOI 10.1109/CBS46900.2019.9114512

Award · 3rd place · NC State ECE Graduate Research Symposium, Nov 2018

Cloud Enabled Autonomous Wheelchair for MND Patients

Poster on a smart power wheelchair for patients with motor neuron disease: a cloud-based neural network that classifies the patient's accent for voice control, direction-of-arrival tracking of voice commands, and Kinect and LiDAR for obstacle avoidance and indoor navigation. The top posters went on to represent the ECE department at NC State's university-wide Graduate Student Research Symposium in March 2019.
